# Findings and evidence index

This index accompanies the [long manuscript](manuscript.pdf). The integrated synthesis states the current interpretation; frozen notes preserve the source history, including limitations and superseded claims. Results with different clocks, priors, truths, or comparators are not pooled into one experiment.

| Finding | Evidence and scope |
| --- | --- |
| A graph covariance for transient shocks and an energy restriction on permanent means are separate assumptions. | [Spatial/general-AR source](evidence/spatiotemporal_bandits/README.md); known stable dynamics and fixed unknown means. |
| Adaptive selected feedback admits exact predictable innovation regression, confidence, contrast stopping, and a conservative sublinear mean-UCB bound. | [Full derivations](appendices/spatiotemporal_bandits.tex), [policy definitions](evidence/spatiotemporal_bandits/policies.md). The certified rule differs from practical tuned UCB. |
| A current-state oracle sees unpredictable innovations; expected regret has a positive linear floor under nondegenerate innovation contrasts. | Spatial/general-AR appendix and [predictive AR(1) source](evidence/predictive_ar1/README.md). The floor is not generally attainable by sparse observation. |
| Graph energy changes the admissible alternatives; resistance geometry can distinguish paths and cliques with equal diameter. | [Graph-alignment source](evidence/alignment_paper/README.md), [novelty audit](evidence/alignment_paper/novelty_audit.md), and full appendix. Finite-sample geometry-policy overhead can dominate. |
| Similarity-index smoothing can reverse the true ranking and cause linear regret if bias is ignored. | [Research note](evidence/notes/graph_spectral_bandits.md) and [counterexample code](evidence/code/s_index_counterexample.py). |
| Certified pooling extends through valid joint confidence, but converting information into sample counts needs schedule-specific growth. | Main manuscript's bridge section. It corrects the per-pull component-size factor and partial-feedback precision in the [historical bridge note](evidence/correlated_arms/README.md). |
| Fixed-design Gaussian PCS has tractable formulas; special two-arm AR(1) schedules and Bayesian/terminal allocations have exact optima. | [Two-arm source](evidence/two_arm_ar1/README.md) and complete appendix. Fixed-truth and prior-averaged theorems remain distinguished. |
| Higher-order AR can make old observations more informative than newer ones. Multi-arm rested-stream error exponents admit a scalar-root allocation solution. | [General-AR allocation source](evidence/ar_p_bandits/README.md). A rested allocation is not a finite-budget restless optimum. |
| The best policy depends on the objective and persistence; short-horizon control is not uniformly best at high persistence. | [AR(1) policy-improvement findings](evidence/predictive_ar1/results/policy_improvement/findings.md). Known means and oracle coefficient comparisons. |
| Fixed-truth 100-arm AR(20) policies show different leaders for current reward, stationary mean regret and PCS. | [Primary controlled findings](evidence/spatiotemporal_bandits/results/policies_ar20/findings.md), 32 paired worlds, five configurations, 18 policies/benchmarks, 1,000 decisions. |
| General AR(2)/AR(3) comparisons verify an exact schedule contrast and show dependence-sensitive selection. | [General-AR findings](evidence/spatiotemporal_bandits/results/general_ar/findings.md). A comparison of two schedules is not a global policy optimum. |
| A random-mean toy provides supplemental evidence; tuning UCB on reported runs biases its headline estimate. | [Toy summary](evidence/st_toy/results/summary.md) and coverage CSVs in the same directory. This is a different Bayesian experiment from fixed-truth AR(20). |
| Bursty exposure breaks iid uncertainty at high persistence; valid innovation certificates can be too conservative to eliminate arms. | [Coverage result records](evidence/st_toy/results/coverage_runs_elim_long.csv) and main synthesis. Scalar independent-shock certificates have narrower assumptions. |
| KuaiRec static smoothing gains largely reflect graph-free shrinkage; graph alignment alone does not guarantee safe decisions. | [Application overview](evidence/kuairec_graphs/README.md), [Setting A](evidence/kuairec_graphs/results/setting_a_table.md), [Setting B](evidence/kuairec_graphs/results/setting_b_table.md), graph alignment tables and nulls. |
| KuaiRec daily temporal modelling is promising but its cached graphs include future data and the cohort includes zero-play cells. | [Daily run records](evidence/kuairec_graphs/results/daily_runs.csv), [executed audit](evidence/st_applications/results/feasibility/findings.md). The existing pilot is exploratory, rather than a corrected chronological evaluation. |
| A second real application fits general AR dynamics with historical graphs on a laptop. | [Wikipedia protocol](experiment_design.md), [completed findings](results/wikipedia/findings.md), [run records](results/wikipedia/runs.csv), [provenance](data/wikipedia/manifest.json). Joint UCB helps some five-reading comparisons; topology adds little over rewiring. |

The [source manifest](evidence_manifest.json) records fingerprints for source documents, compact results, and scripts. Large raw simulation files can be referenced by fingerprints without being duplicated. The [package verification](verification.json) identifies which copies match the collected versions. Concurrent alternative compiled papers stored as source context are not independent experiments added to the synthesized evidence.
